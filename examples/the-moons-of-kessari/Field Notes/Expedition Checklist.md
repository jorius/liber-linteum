# Expedition Checklist

*Started the night of the 15th Closing of 413. Last ticked on the afternoon of the 17th, at the top of the last ridge.*

## Before the road

- [x] Copy the Rise rows of 396 to 398 from the stacks, ink and knots side by side
- [x] Knot a message for Mother. **Not ink.**
- [x] Two spools of plain cord, one of good
- [x] Pens, an ink cake, three strips of clean linen
- [x] Satchel, robe, the sandals that don't rub
- [x] Find the quartermaster before the camels wake
- [ ] Tell anyone else

## On the road

- [x] Find out what *Varn business* is: [[Hollow Stone]], half a day east. A glass pipe, and water **running**
- [x] Watch the quartermaster's fingers at the scale. Keepers' knots!
- [x] The [[Cairn of Teeth]]
- [x] Six years of cask cords from Dela. Keep them dry. Keep them *close*

## Glassreach

- [x] The Annex: the twin of 396 to 398. Its ink agrees with its cord
- [x] A copy of the Rise rows, with the Annex witness knot
- [x] Ask after the twin of 399 to 401: never came. A mourning knot on the arrivals cord
- [x] Be at the yards by noon. Dela turns round at noon
- [ ] Sleep

## Back at the Courts: the Dusk Closing

- [x] Be over the last ridge before the moons rise
- [ ] Show Mother everything. All of it. In order.
- [ ] Someone has to go down the well at the Closing. Pell has the key.
- [ ] Measuring cord, sixty spans, a knot at every span
- [ ] Lamp, and spare oil
- [ ] The sitting at low moon. *The Keeper's reading is the Keeper's.*
