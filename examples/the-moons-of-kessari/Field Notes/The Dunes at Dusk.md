# The Dunes at Dusk

Press play, and turn it up: most of it is below the range of small speakers.

![[the-dunes-at-dusk.wav]]

> [!note] About the recording
> There are no real singing dunes on Kessari, so this clip is synthesised: a low drone with a slow wobble and a grainy edge, two more "dunes" joining in, and wind made from filtered noise. Nothing in it was recorded from the world. The script that makes it, and every image in this vault, is `examples/scripts/make_kessari_assets.py`.

## What to listen for

| Time | What you hear |
|---:|---|
| 0:00 | Wind only, gusting |
| 0:02 | The first dune begins: a low hum, rising slowly |
| 0:07 | A second, lower dune joins underneath. They don't quite agree, and the air beats between them |
| 0:12 | A third, higher voice answers for a few seconds. Glassreach, the drovers would say |
| 0:17 | The voices fall away one by one |
| 0:20 | Wind again, and then nothing |

## Ama's note

*Dawn of the 16th Closing, the short one. Glassreach, the north gate.*

The spires started humming while I was crossing the market, so I went out through the north gate to hear where it comes from. It starts in your teeth before your ears. Then in the sand under your feet. Then everywhere. The drovers at the gate pretended not to notice; the camels didn't bother pretending. ==I found I was counting it==, the way you count knots, as if the dunes were reading something out in a language made of one note.

The long Closing is in thirteen and a half days. The water will come up the shaft under the Courts while this is going on, and I want to be down there, watching it, when it does. #closing #sound
