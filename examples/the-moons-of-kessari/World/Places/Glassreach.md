# Glassreach

**Glassreach** is the largest city on Kessari and the strangest. Long before the Well, something fell burning onto the plain the drovers call the **Glass Flats** and burned for a very long time; when it went out, the sand for half a day's walk in every direction had become glass, heaved up into spires and blades and long ridges like frozen surf. The first Glassreachers did not build a city. They moved into one, and carved doors.

## The city

- **The Spires.** The tall glass towers, where the houses' agents and the richer merchants live, high up where the wind comes through.
- **The Lees.** The low quarter among the roots of the spires, where most people live. The light arrives there broken into colours by a hundred thicknesses of glass, and the water lines are long.
- **The Glass Market.** A great open floor of black glass at the city's heart, where everything on Kessari is sold, including, until 413, water that nobody could explain.
- **The gates.** The Salt Road leaves from the north gate. Caravans arrive at dusk, to spare the camels the heat.

## The hum

At every Closing, when the dunes of [[The Singing Erg]] begin to sing, the spires of Glassreach take up the note and ring with it, a deep clean hum that you feel in your teeth. For the length of a long breath, everyone in the city stops what they are doing and stands with their head a little bowed.

> [!quote] Ama Seyru, in [[04 Glassreach]]
> She had been told about it and had not believed it.

## Water

Glassreach has no well. It lives on the **allotment** the [[The Salt Courts|Courts]] send down the Salt Road every fortnight, on dew scraped from shaded walls before dawn, and on whatever can be bought. The allotment was cut three times between 402 and 412, and a fourth cut, of one part in five, was proposed in 413. It was struck instead, and the allotment restored. #water
