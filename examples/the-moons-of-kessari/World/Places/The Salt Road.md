# The Salt Road

The **Salt Road** runs from the north gate of [[Glassreach]] to [[The Salt Courts]], nine days for a rider and twelve for a loaded caravan. It is not really a road. Any road on Kessari would be buried in a week. It is a line of **cairns**, one every few hundred paces, each stone set by some caravan long ago and reset by every caravan since, so that the way is always there even when the ground is not.

![[map-of-the-salt-road.png]]

## Stages

Counted from the Courts, southbound, as a loaded caravan walks it:

| Day | Stage | Notes |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | [[The Salt Courts]] | The yards are below the Terraces, by the well. Caravans leave before the sun |
| ½ | [[Hollow Stone]] | A Varn waystation half a day east of the cairns; Varn caravans detour to it on the first evening |
| 4 | [[Cairn of Teeth]] | Black rock, shade, a place to water the camels |
| 6 | Dry Fork | The track to the salt pans branches west |
| 9 | Salt Ninth | The last white pan, named for the ninth day out |
| 12 | [[Glassreach]], north gate | Caravans arrive at dusk |

Riders do the same in nine days, and Varn's wind-skiffs, when the wind is right, in five.

## Caravans

A Salt Road caravan is forty camels, twenty-odd drovers, a caravan master who decides where to camp and a **quartermaster** who decides everything else: what is carried, what is weighed, what is owed. Southbound the casks carry the Glassreach allotment and whatever the houses are selling; northbound they carry salt, glass, and empty casks, which travel light.

> [!tip] The quartermaster's rule
> *Weigh it. Knot it. Never trust a number you didn't tie yourself.* Taught to [[Dela Fenn]] by [[Ilen Seyru]], on the road, in the Dry Year.

## Dangers

- **Sandfall.** A dune face that lets go all at once. Quick, they say.
- **Heat.** Caravans travel at dawn and dusk and lie up through the middle of the day.
- **Being alone.** No one walks the road alone. *The road takes whom it takes*, the drovers used to say. Since 413 they say it less.
