# Well of Osk

The **Well of Osk** is a round shaft cut straight down through the rock beside the Courts, wide enough for six people to stand side by side on its stair, and deep enough that a dropped pebble takes three breaths to be heard. At its bottom lies the only water on Kessari that does not have to be scraped off glass or squeezed out of a plant: the deep water, which comes and goes with the moons.

## The shaft

The first wellwrights reached water in YW 1, and spent the next eight years lining the shaft with blocks of glass carried up the Salt Road from the Flats and fused in place. The glass is the reason the well has lasted. It does not crumble, it does not leach salt, and it remembers: wherever the high water has stood, it leaves a fine white line of salt on the glass, and four hundred years of Closings have laid those lines down in a band a little below the stair's upper landings.

A spiral stair is cut into the lining all the way down. At its head is an iron gate, and the key hangs on the belt of the Master Wellwright, at present [[Pell Odrin]].

## The readings

| Reading | When | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Low-moon level** | At every sitting of the Courts | How much water is standing in the well |
| **The Rise** | Every Closing | How much water the deep brought up since the last Closing |
| **Draws** | Every day | What each house took, written as a debt by the Keepers |

Before the Dry Year, the wellwrights went down at every Closing and scratched the high water on the glass, and the Rise was *measured*. Since 401 it has been *reckoned* instead, from the low-moon level and the recorded draws:

> Rise = (level now − level at the last sitting) + all recorded draws since

which is perfectly sound arithmetic, so long as every draw is recorded.

> [!warning] Going down at a Closing
> At a Closing the water rises fast and without warning, and the lower stair floods. From 401 to 413 the Courts forbade anyone, wellwrights included, to go down while the moons were together. Since the sitting of 413 the stair has stood open at every Dusk Closing to anyone who wishes to look, under the wellwrights' eye. Stay above the chalk line, and climb when they tell you to.

> [!info]- Spoilers: the hole in the glass
> On the night of the Dusk Closing of 413, [[Ama Seyru]], [[Mother Hesk]] and [[Pell Odrin]] watched the water rise to within a finger of the old salt line, proving the well was not failing. Just below the line they found a round bore cut through the glass, which drew off the high water at every Closing and carried it to a Varn cistern at [[Hollow Stone]]. Because it drew only at high water, no draw was ever recorded and the low-moon readings simply showed a well that seemed to be dying. The wellwrights sealed the bore with new glass before the next Closing. Pell's scratch beside the salt line reads *Dusk Closing 413. P.O. saw it.* See [[05 The Closing]].
