# Cairn of Teeth

Three or four days south of the Courts, the Salt Road passes a place where black rock breaks out of the sand in a long, jagged line, like the jaw of something enormous lying just under the dunes. The rocks give shade, and the caravans stop there at midday to water the camels. The biggest cairn on the whole road stands at the end of the line, where the last tooth is.

It is a place people prefer not to stay long. The black rock holds the heat into the night, the sand between the teeth drifts deep, and the slip faces on its southern side have a reputation.
