# The Glass Annex

Every ledger the [[The Linen-Keepers|Linen-Keepers]] make, they make twice. One copy stays in [[The Loom House]] at the Courts. The other, the **twin**, is carried down the Salt Road to the **Glass Annex**, a vault cut into the roots of the oldest spire in [[Glassreach]], so that no single fire, flood or thief can ever take the whole of the Courts' memory.

The Annex is reached by a long stair, and it grows colder with every step, until at the bottom you can see your breath. The cold and the still air are kind to linen; rolls from the Keepers' very first year are kept there, and still fold like sleeves.

## The arrivals cord

Every twin that arrives is recorded by a knot on the **arrivals cord**, a single unbroken cord that runs round all four walls of the reading room, one knot per roll, in the order they came. A twin that is lost on the road gets a *mourning knot* instead: a loose knot, deliberately untidy, so that a reader running the cord through their fingers feels it at once.

There is a mourning knot for the 17th Closing of 401.

## Keepers of the Annex

For most of the last forty years the Annex has been kept by **Sefa Imre**, who is half blind and reads a cord faster than most Keepers read ink. Since 413 she has had help. See [[Mother Hesk]].

> [!tip] A witness knot
> When the Annex copies a twin for someone, the keeper ties a short, fat *witness knot* into the copy's cord. It means: this copy was made from the twin, here, by me. It is the Annex's only seal, and it has never been forged.
