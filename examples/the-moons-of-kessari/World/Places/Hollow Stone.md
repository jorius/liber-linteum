# Hollow Stone

**Hollow Stone** is an outcrop of black rock rising out of the dunes half a day east of the Salt Road, on the first night out from the Courts. It looks, the drovers say, like the knuckles of a buried fist. In YW 250 the Courts granted it to [[House Varn]] as a waystation, and for a hundred and fifty years it was exactly that: a stone post, a well-shaded camel yard, a flag.

Southbound caravans that carry for Varn detour to it on their first evening. It is called *Varn business*, and nobody asks about it.

> [!info]- Spoilers: what comes out of the rock
> Since the Dry Year, a pipe of thick green glass has come out of the rock at Hollow Stone at the height of a man's chest, and at every Closing water pours out of it: not dripping but *running*, in a rope as thick as a wrist. It is the far end of a bore that [[Orla Varn]] had reopened in 401, running from the outcrop to the shaft of the [[Well of Osk]], just below its high-water line. At every Closing the risen water spilled into the bore and ran down to Hollow Stone, where it was sealed into casks with blue salt and sent south to be sold in [[Glassreach]] as *sweetwater*.
>
> [[Dela Fenn]] weighed every one of those casks for six years. See [[03 The Salt Road]] and [[Knot Reckoning]].
>
> The bore was sealed in 413. The pipe at Hollow Stone is dry, and somebody has hung a long knot on it.
