# Places

The inhabited part of Kessari is a single line drawn across the sand: the [[The Salt Road|Salt Road]], running nine days from [[Glassreach]] in the south to the Courts and the Well in the north. Almost everything that matters happens at one end of it, or the other, or somewhere along the way.

![[map-of-the-salt-road.png]]

## At the Courts

| Place | What it is |
|---|---|
| [[Well of Osk]] | The one deep well on Kessari, lined with glass, rising at every Closing |
| [[The Salt Courts]] | The terraced hall where the seven houses sit at low moon |
| [[The Loom House]] | Home and archive of the Linen-Keepers, beside the well |

## Along the road

| Place | What it is |
|---|---|
| [[The Salt Road]] | Cairns, caravans, and nine to twelve days of sand |
| [[Cairn of Teeth]] | A ridge of black rock, four days out; where Ilen Seyru was lost |
| [[Hollow Stone]] | A Varn waystation half a day east of the road |
| [[The Singing Erg]] | The great dune sea south of the road, which sings at the Closing |

## At Glassreach

| Place | What it is |
|---|---|
| [[Glassreach]] | A city of sand fused into glass |
| [[The Glass Annex]] | The cold vault under the oldest spire where the twin ledgers are kept |
