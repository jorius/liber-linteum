# The Loom House

The **Loom House** is the home, workshop and archive of [[The Linen-Keepers]]: a long, low building of pale stone beside the [[Well of Osk]], built round a courtyard strung with drying lines. It was raised in YW 15, the year the Keepers were charged with recording every water debt, and it has been extended so many times since that the newest wing is a quarter of a mile from the oldest.

## Inside

- **The stacks.** Racks of rolled ledgers, floor to ceiling, room after room, each roll on its own wooden roller. The house is never quite silent; the linen shifts as the air changes, and at night it sounds like sleepers turning over.
- **The looms.** The Keepers weave their own linen, a tight, even cloth with a corded selvedge for the knots. The big loom in the hall is older than the house.
- **The bench.** Where apprentices learn to read knots blindfolded, and to tell which way a pen was moving from the weight of the ink.

## The airing

On every Closing night, when the damp breath of the deep comes up the well and through the town, the Keepers carry every ledger out into the courtyard and hang it on the lines to drink. Dry linen folded too often splits along its creases. Linen that has breathed a little folds like a sleeve.

From outside the wall, on an airing night, the courtyard looks as if it has filled with patient ghosts.

> [!quote] The first thing every apprentice is taught
> Paper cracks in the dry. Parchment curls, and the wind carries off ash. Only linen remembers.
