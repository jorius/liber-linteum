# Ilen Seyru

| | |
|---|---|
| **Born** | YW 369 |
| **Lost** | 17th Closing of 401, at the [[Cairn of Teeth]] |
| **Rank** | Keeper; kept the roll of 399 to 401 |
| **Cord** | A blue so grey it is nearly the colour of linen |

**Ilen Seyru** was a Keeper of [[The Loom House]] and the mother of [[Ama Seyru]]. People who knew her remember a tall woman who laughed at her own jokes, wrote her sevens with a hook like the neck of a heron, and could not leave a sum alone until it came out right.

In the Dry Year she set out down the Salt Road towards [[The Glass Annex]] with the twin of the roll of 399 to 401, early, before the roll was even finished. She wanted it somewhere safe, she told Mother Hesk, and would not say why. Every night of the journey she went to look at House Varn's sealed casks. On the fourth night, at the Cairn of Teeth, she went out and did not come back.

## The letter

Ama still has the only letter Ilen ever wrote her, nine lines on a strip of linen, sent up the road when Ama was six. The ink has worn pale under Ama's thumb. She has never told anyone what it says.

## What she taught

> [!quote] To a sixteen-year-old cask-girl, at the scale, in the Dry Year
> If you're going to be nosy, child, be nosy properly. Weigh it. Knot it. Never trust a number you didn't tie yourself.

The cask-girl was [[Dela Fenn]], and she did exactly that, for six years.
