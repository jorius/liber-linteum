# Orla Varn

| | |
|---|---|
| **Born** | YW 338 |
| **House** | <span data-color="blue">**Varn**</span>, head of house since 399 |
| **At the Courts** | Sits with her hands folded on her stick and her eyes half shut; rarely speaks |
| **Now** | Comes to every sitting and sits beside her dark stool |

**Orla Varn** became head of [[House Varn]] in 399, on the death of her husband Matthias, two years before the Dry Year. People respect her the way people respect a sandstorm. At the Courts she lets her son [[Teodor Varn]] speak for the house, and in the whole of [[The Story]] she says only a handful of sentences. They are enough.

> [!info]- Spoilers: the Dry Year
> When the Rise failed in 401, Orla reopened an old bore from [[Hollow Stone]] to the shaft of the [[Well of Osk]], so that her house could draw the high water at every Closing without anyone recording it. She moved in the Courts to forbid going down the well at a Closing. Eleven days after [[Ilen Seyru]] was lost at the [[Cairn of Teeth]] she brought Ilen's twin ledger to [[Mother Hesk]], and made sure Hesk understood what could happen to a child on the Salt Road. Asked at the sitting of 413 to answer for it, she said:
>
> *When the water fell in the Dry Year, I saw the end of everything. Every one of you did. I was simply the one who did something about it. I made sure my house would outlive the well. Then the water came back. And it seemed such a waste to tell anyone.*
