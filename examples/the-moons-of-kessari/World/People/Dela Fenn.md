# Dela Fenn

**Dela Fenn** is quartermaster of a Salt Road caravan and of the caravan yards below the Terraces: a short, sun-cured woman in her late twenties with a slate on a cord round her neck and a voice that never stops counting. She started on the road at sixteen as a cask-girl, in the Dry Year, on the caravan that lost [[Ilen Seyru]] at the [[Cairn of Teeth]], and the Keeper's rule has been hers ever since: *weigh it, knot it, never trust a number you didn't tie yourself*.

For six years she weighed every sealed Varn cask that went through her yards and tied the weights into a cord, in Keepers' knots, without ever breaking a seal or asking what was under it. In [[03 The Salt Road]] she gave the whole bag of cords to Ilen's daughter. In [[06 What the Glass Remembers]] she stood in the doorway of the Courts with her slate and said, *I count casks.*
