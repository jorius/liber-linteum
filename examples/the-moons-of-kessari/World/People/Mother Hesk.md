# Mother Hesk

| | |
|---|---|
| **Born** | YW 335 |
| **Rank** | Eldest of [[The Loom House]], 371 to 413 |
| **Cord** | Ochre, with the Eldest's black thread |
| **Now** | In [[Glassreach]], at [[The Glass Annex]] |

**Mother Hesk** ran the Loom House for forty-two years: a small, straight-backed woman with a small, upright hand, who touched every ledger as if it were a living thing and never raised her voice. She taught most of the Keepers now living to read a cord, including [[Ilen Seyru]] and, after Ilen was lost, Ilen's daughter [[Ama Seyru]], whom she raised.

> [!quote] To Ama, in [[02 Low Moon]]
> Your mother found out that the world is unjust, and she thought it could be argued with. Go to bed.

> [!info]- Spoilers: the year of nights
> Eleven days after Ilen was lost, [[Orla Varn]] came to the Loom House with the twin Ilen had been carrying, set it on Hesk's table, and observed that children are lost on the Salt Road every year. Ama was seven, and asleep in the next room.
>
> Hesk spent most of the next year rewriting six years of Rise rows in the Loom House copies, in the hands of the Keepers who had written them, so that the well seemed to have been failing slowly since 396. Orla watched her burn the twin. She did not touch the knots. *I told myself that was courage. It was only a smaller cowardice.*
>
> When Ama found the knots, Hesk told her to put it away. When Ama came back up the Salt Road with proof, Hesk confessed to her, took her down the well at the Closing, and the next morning confessed again before the whole of the Courts and asked that her name be struck from the Loom House. It was. She went down the road to Glassreach with Dela Fenn's caravan a week later, and now spends her mornings at the Annex with Sefa Imre and her afternoons teaching knots to children in the Lees. Her letters to Ama are knotted, never written.

## Letters

Three of the knotted letters Ama keeps on the peg above her bed, read with her fingers:

1. *Forty children here can read a cord. Three of them better than you.*
2. *Sefa says your knots lean like your mother's. Tell her I know.*
3. *Found the girl from the dew wall. Her name is Tali. She counts everything. Dela wants her for a cask-girl. Over my body.*
