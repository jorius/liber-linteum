# Pell Odrin

| | |
|---|---|
| **Born** | YW 337 |
| **Guild** | [[The Wellwrights]]; Master since 385 |
| **Carries** | The key to the gate of the well-stair, a coil of measuring line, a diamond stylus |

**Pell Odrin** has kept the [[Well of Osk]] for longer than most people at the Courts have been alive. Before the Dry Year he went down at every Closing and scratched the high water on the glass, as his master had and his master's master. Since 401 he has stood up at every sitting and given the low-moon reading in a voice like a rope running over a pulley, and the reading has always been true, and it has always been only half the truth.

> [!quote] At the gate, in [[05 The Closing]]
> It's my well. I'll decide who goes down it.

> [!quote] At the sitting, in [[06 What the Glass Remembers]]
> I'm an old man, and memory isn't linen, and nobody asked me.

## His mark

Beside the salt line in the shaft, a finger above the water's edge on the night of the Dusk Closing of 413, Pell scratched a line and wrote beside it:

```text
Dusk Closing 413. P.O. saw it.
```

It is the first high-water mark scratched in the well for twelve years. There have been thirteen more since.
