**Ama Seyru** is the daughter of the Keeper [[Ilen Seyru]], who was lost on the Salt Road when Ama was seven. She was brought up in [[The Loom House]] by its Eldest, [[Mother Hesk]], and has never lived anywhere else. She was the fastest knot-reader of her year, which she is not proud of, exactly, but is not *not* proud of either.

## Character

Quiet, exact, stubborn in the way water is stubborn: she does not push, she simply keeps going until something gives. She thinks with her hands. When she is upset she counts the cord at her cuff; when she has decided something, her fingers have usually decided it first.

She has her mother's laugh, [[Dela Fenn]] says, though she didn't use it once on the whole of the Salt Road. And she makes her sevens the way her mother did, with a little hook at the top like the neck of a heron, pulled quickly from right to left. She didn't know that until the last page of chapter six.
