# The Seven Houses

In YW 14, seven families who between them owned the camels, the salt pans, the glass-cutters and the lamps of the young settlement at Osk swore the **Salt Compact**: the well would belong to all of them and to none, every draw would be a debt written on linen, and they would decide everything together, at low moon. Four hundred years later the same seven houses still sit in [[The Salt Courts]], each on its stool, each in its colour.

## Allotments

Each house is allotted a share of the well's Rise at every Closing, and draws against it; whatever it draws is written by [[The Linen-Keepers]] as a debt to the other six. Glassreach, which has no house of its own, receives a separate **allotment** voted by all seven. That is the allotment the Courts proposed to cut in 413.
