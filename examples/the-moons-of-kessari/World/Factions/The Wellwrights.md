# The Wellwrights

The **wellwrights** dug the [[Well of Osk]] in YW 1, lined it with glass over the next eight years, and have looked after it ever since. They are a guild, not a house: they own no water and draw none beyond their ration, and they answer to [[The Salt Courts]] as a whole. You can tell a wellwright by the leather apron, the coil of measuring line on the hip, and the diamond stylus on the belt.

## What they do

- **Keep the shaft.** Inspect and repair the glass lining, block by block, from the stair.
- **Keep the gate.** The iron gate at the head of the stair is locked, and the Master Wellwright carries the key.
- **Give the reading.** At every sitting of the Courts the Master Wellwright stands and gives the **low-moon level** and the reckoned **Rise**.
- **Scratch the high water.** Before 401, a wellwright went down at every Closing and scratched the high water on the glass with a diamond stylus: a line, a date, two initials. Four hundred years of those marks crowd the band just under the salt line.

## The prohibition of 401

> [!warning]
> In the Dry Year a young wellwright, Jory Teel, drowned on the lower stair at a Closing. On a motion of [[Orla Varn]], *for the safety of the guild*, the Courts forbade anyone to go down while the moons were together. For twelve years the high water went unmeasured and unseen, and the Rise was reckoned instead of read.

The prohibition was lifted at the sitting of 413. The wellwrights now stand on the stair at every Dusk Closing with lamps and chalk, keeping the public above the line and telling them when to climb.
