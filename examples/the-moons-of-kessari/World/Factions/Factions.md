# Factions

Every dram on Kessari passes through three pairs of hands. The houses **own** it. The wellwrights **measure** it. The Keepers **write it down**. The whole order of the world rests on the three keeping one another honest, and *The Story* is about what happens when two of them stop.

| Faction | Their part | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| [[The Seven Houses]] | Own the [[Well of Osk]] together, under the Salt Compact; draw from it, sell, carry, and vote | Seven, one each |
| [[House Varn]] | The richest of the seven: water carriage, cisterns and wind-skiffs | <span data-bg="blue">Varn blue</span> |
| [[The Linen-Keepers]] | Record every water debt twice, in ink and in knots; read the sums aloud before every sealing | <span data-bg="gray">undyed grey</span> |
| [[The Wellwrights]] | Keep the shaft, the stair and the gate; give the low-moon reading at every sitting | Leather aprons |

> [!note] The Salt Compact, first line
> *The well belongs to all seven houses and to none of them; and the houses shall sit only at low moon, so that none may say the moons favoured it.*
