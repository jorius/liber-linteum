## Kessari in one paragraph

Water is wealth, and it is counted in **drams**. All of it that matters comes up from the deep under the [[Well of Osk]], which the seven merchant houses of [[The Salt Courts]] govern together. Whatever a house draws, it owes, and the debt is written on linen by [[The Linen-Keepers]], because paper cracks in the dry. Twice in every turn of the pale moon, [[Ilu]] and [[Thane]] rise together; the deep water rises with them, and the dunes sing. People call it the Closing. #worldbuilding
