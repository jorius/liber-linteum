# Thane

**Thane** is the smaller and nearer of [[Kessari]]'s moons: a <span data-color="red">rust-red</span> disc a third the apparent size of [[Ilu]], which goes round the world in $T_{\text{Thane}} = 9$ days, three times as fast as the old moon.

## In the sky

Thane moves fast enough to notice. Over a single night it visibly slides against the stars, it rises nearly three hours later each night than the night before, and drovers tell the time of night by how far it has run. Its light is weak and coloured; on a Thane-only night the dunes look like banked embers, and everything casts a faint red shadow.

Because it laps Ilu twice in every one of her turns, the two moons meet every

$$
S = \left(\frac{1}{9} - \frac{1}{27}\right)^{-1} = 13.5 \text{ days,}
$$

which is the **Closing**. See [[Kessari]] for the whole calculation.

## Why it is red

Nobody knows. The Wellwrights hold that Thane is made of the same iron-rich rock as the black outcrops of the [[Cairn of Teeth]]; the drovers hold that it is embarrassed about always being late.

> [!tip] Spotting the Closing
> Look east at dusk. If [[Ilu]] is just rising and Thane is a hand's width above the horizon and *gaining*, the moons will meet within two nights.
