# Moons

Kessari has two moons, and they could hardly be less alike. Children learn them as a pair: *Ilu walks, Thane runs*.

::::columns
:::column
### 🌕 [[Ilu]]

- Large, pale, slow
- Once round in **27 days**
- Rises a little later every night, like a tired old woman
- Thirteen turns make a year
- Drovers navigate by her
:::
:::column
### 🔴 [[Thane]]

- Small, <span data-color="red">red</span>, fast
- Once round in **9 days**
- Rises nearly three hours later every night, always catching up
- Three of his turns to one of hers
- Drovers tell the time by him
:::
::::

## When they rise together

Thane overtakes Ilu every **13½ days**, and on those nights the two moons come up over the dunes side by side. That is the **Closing**: the deep water rises in the [[Well of Osk]], the dunes of [[The Singing Erg]] sing, and the Linen-Keepers hang every ledger in the courtyard to drink the damp. The maths is on the [[Kessari]] page.

> [!quote] Drovers' saying
> When Ilu and Thane walk together, mind your water: the deep is listening.
