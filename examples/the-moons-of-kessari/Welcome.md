# Welcome

This is **The Moons of Kessari**, a sample vault for **Liber Linteum**. It holds a short novel in six chapters and an epilogue, the world it is set in, and the notes one of its characters kept along the way. Everything in it, the words, the pictures and the sound, was made for this vault.

The app is named for the *liber linteus*, the "linen book" of the ancient world, written on cloth because cloth outlasts most things. The story is about people who keep their records the same way, and what happens when someone rewrites them.

> [!tip] How to read this vault
> 1. **Start with the story.** Open [[01 The Linen-Keeper's Daughter]] and follow the links at the foot of each chapter. About forty minutes, start to finish.
> 2. **Then wander.** Open the **Global View** and walk the map of covers: from [[The Story]] into the [[World]], from a person to the place they were lost, from a house to the secret it kept. The lines between cards are the relationships each page declares.
> 3. **Then read the margins.** [[Field Notes]] holds Ama's own working notes, written in the middle of the story.

## Bring your own

- **Obsidian vaults open as they are.** Liber Linteum reads plain Markdown files with wikilinks, embeds, callouts, tags and YAML frontmatter, and writes them back the same way. This very folder opens in Obsidian too; only the columns and coloured text, which Obsidian doesn't know, show up there as plain text.
- **Notion imports.** `examples/notion-sample-export.zip`, next to this vault, is a Notion export of a caravan quartermaster's workspace. (You have met her: [[Dela Fenn]].) Import it to see pages, databases and toggles arrive as Markdown.

> [!note] How this vault was made
> The covers, the map and the sound of the dunes are procedural, generated by `examples/scripts/make_kessari_assets.py` with Python and Pillow: no photographs, samples or borrowed art.
