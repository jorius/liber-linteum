Liber Linteum: app icon concepts

12 directions. Each one is shown at 128 px, in a light and a dark Dock at 40 px, and at 18 px (Finder sidebar and tabs). Colours use the app's terracotta, plum, ink and a linen cream. Every concept can be recoloured. Click any you like (several is fine), then tell me in the terminal what to keep, combine or change.

1 · Monogram

Woven LL

Two Ls, linen and terracotta, crossed like warp and weft. Bold and readable small.

2 · Book

Linen-bound book

A notebook with a woven-linen cover and a spine. The literal "linen book", warm and familiar.

3 · Book

Folded linen

A strip of cloth folded into an open book. A nod to the Etruscan book, which was folded linen.

4 · Canvas

Canvas & stitch

A page on a linen background, signed off with a running stitch and a needle. "Lienzo", plus craft.

5 · Script

Ancient script

Abstract archaic letters in red ink on ruled linen, like the original artefact. Beautiful at 128 px, busy at 18 px.

6 · Codex

Codex stack

Three stacked pages in the palette's colours with a bookmark ribbon. Many notes, one place.

7 · Weave

Loom

Warp threads with two weft ribbons running through: ideas woven together. Abstract and distinctive.

8 · Minimal

Ribbon L

A bookmark ribbon bent into an L across a blank page. Quiet and modern, close to the macOS Notes style.

9 · Book

Open book

A simple open book on terracotta. Instantly readable at any size, but the least distinctive.

10 · Scroll

Linen roll

A roll of linen unrolling into a page of notes, on ink. Old-world feel, still clean.

11 · Thread

Thread L

A single thread drawing an L that loops into a knot, with two plum "notes" at its ends. Linked notes, made of thread.

12 · Seal

Wax seal

A terracotta seal stamped with an L on linen. Private, sealed: fits the encryption story.
