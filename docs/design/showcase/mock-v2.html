What it is

A notebook that stays yours

Plain Markdown, your folders

Every note is a .md file you can open anywhere. Wikilinks, frontmatter and folders, exactly as you left them.

See it as a map

The Global View shows projects, folders and pages as cards with covers, linked by what they reference. Drill in, or expand in place.

Private by design

No servers to trust: the app makes no network requests at all. Optional encrypted vaults for what should stay sealed.

NEW SECTION · OBSIDIAN + NOTION
Bring your notes

Leave the cloud, keep your notes

Moving to something private shouldn't mean starting over. Point Liber Linteum at the notes you already have, and they stay where they are: on your disk.

Opening an Obsidian vault · looping video
Obsidian

Open your vault as it is

Choose the folder and you're in. Nothing is converted, copied or moved; Obsidian and Liber Linteum can share the same vault.

  • [[Wikilinks]], embeds, callouts, tags and frontmatter
  • Your attachment folder settings are respected
  • Its settings folder is left untouched
Importing a Notion export · looping video
Notion

Import your workspace

Export from Notion as Markdown & CSV, then give Liber Linteum the zip. It's read on your machine; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Pages and nested pages keep their structure and links
  • Databases become a page with a table, one note per row
  • Callouts and toggles become their Markdown equivalents
Your notes move from a service you rent to a folder you own. Try it with the sample Notion export in the repo.

Privacy

What it will never do

These aren't settings. The app is built so it can't: network access is blocked by its security policy, refused by its permissions, banned in its code and kept out of its dependencies.

No cloudYour notes live in a folder on your disk. Sync them with whatever you already trust, or don't.
No accountsNothing to sign up for, nothing to log into, nothing to lose access to.
No telemetryNo analytics, no crash reports, no "anonymous usage data". Zero requests, checked by tests.
