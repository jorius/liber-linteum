Your own accent

Proposed Settings feature

Pick any colour. The app builds the whole palette from it: the accent is darkened for light mode and lightened for dark mode until text in that colour stays readable (WCAG AA, 4.5:1), and the backgrounds and greys take a faint tint of the same hue. Try a very light yellow or a near-black: the contrast numbers below stay above the line.

How the other feedback items look in the mock

← Back arrow

Appears left of the breadcrumb only after you followed an internal link, and goes back along the links you followed (⌘[ also works; ⌘] goes forward). Opening a page from the explorer starts a fresh trail, so the arrow disappears.

Editable title

Click the title under the breadcrumb to edit it. Enter or clicking away renames the file and updates every link to it; Escape cancels. Names that can't be file names are explained inline.

Formatting toolbar

Bold, italic, strikethrough, highlight, code, link · heading level · bullet, numbered and task list, quote, divider. Shows the active styles at the cursor. Hidden or shown from the page header or Settings (remembered); ⌘B, ⌘I, ⌘⇧X and the rest work either way.

Fonts in Settings

Separate pickers for Live Preview and for Source, plus text size. Bundled fonts work offline; any font installed on your computer can be picked too. Nothing is downloaded.
