# Naming brief: a new name for "Notes In My Way"

**Chosen (2026-10-20): Liber Linteum.**

**Sources covered:** Tolkien's Elvish, Latin, Greek, Chibcha (Muisca) and Inca (Quechua and Aymara).

## How to read this

- **Research date:** 2026-10-20.
- **Meanings** come from these sources:
  - Elvish: [Eldamo](https://eldamo.org/), a lexicon that cites Tolkien's own manuscripts.
  - Latin: Lewis & Short, read on Perseus.
  - Greek: LSJ (Liddell–Scott–Jones), read on Perseus.
  - Muisca:
    - the 17th-century *Diccionario y gramática chibcha*, edited by María Stella González de Pérez (Instituto Caro y Cuervo, 1987), [as digitised in the Intercontinental Dictionary Series (IDS)](https://ids.clld.org/sources/perez1987);
    - plus a 2020 study of Muisca words for memory.
  - Aymara: IDS, contributed by Félix Layme Pairumani.
  - Quechua: the sources named in each row.
- **Domains** are written as `.com·.app·.dev`, where **T** = registered and **F** = no registration found. They come from registry lookups (RDAP) on 2026-10-20. "F" can still be a reserved or premium domain.
- **Collisions** come from quick web searches. **Nothing here is a trademark clearance.** Before you commit, search USPTO, EUIPO, SIC (Colombia) and IMPI (Mexico).
- **Pronunciation:** EN is for English speakers and ES for Spanish speakers. Stressed syllables are in CAPS.

### IP risk

**Who owns what:**
- The **Tolkien Estate** holds the literary copyright.
- **Middle-earth Enterprises** (formerly Tolkien Enterprises, under the Saul Zaentz Company) holds the trademark and merchandising rights to *The Lord of the Rings* and *The Hobbit*.
- **Embracer** bought Middle-earth Enterprises in 2022 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises)).
- In 2025 Embracer renamed the division that holds those rights **Fellowship Entertainment** ([TheOneRing.net](https://www.theonering.net/torwp/2025/06/09/120500-embracer-group-renames-itself-fellowship-entertainment/), [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/embracer-shakes-up-its-gaming-divisions-lord-of-the-rings-group-rebranded-coffee-stain-spins-off)).

**Enforcement history:**
- **2012, The Hobbit pub (Southampton):** threatened over its name, offered a licence at $100 a year, and sent another letter in 2020 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hobbit,_Southampton), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2012/03/16/hobbit_reprieved/)).
- **"Lord of the Wings":** an earlier application that Middle-earth Enterprises blocked.
- **2025, Lord of the Fries (Australia):** Middle-earth Enterprises opposed the mark and lost before IP Australia ([Variety AU](https://au.variety.com/2025/more/news/lord-of-the-fries-trademark-dispute-winner-20840)).
- **2026, a Turkish jeweller:** sued over a ring trademark ([IIPLA](https://iipla.org/news/turkish-jeweler-faces-us-lawsuit-over-lord-of-the-rings-trademark)).
- **2023, the Polychron case:** the Estate won an injunction and about $134k in fees against an unauthorised sequel. That was a copyright case, not a naming case ([report](https://westmeathexaminer.ie/?p=87933)).

**What that means for us:**
- **Ordinary Elvish nouns** like *enyalië*, *sanwë* and *tecil* carry less risk than famous proper names such as Palantír, Mithril or Rivendell.
- I found no public lawsuit against Palantir or Anduril, but that is not clearance.
- **Tengwar** and **Quenta** are the most exposed.
- Ask a lawyer before you file.

## 4. Chibcha (Muisca)

### What the record is

- Muysccubun, the Muisca language, died out around the 17th–18th century.
- Everything we know comes from a few colonial dictionaries, chiefly:
  - the anonymous manuscript edited by González de Pérez (1987), digitised as [IDS](https://ids.clld.org/contributions/243);
  - and the Bogotá missionary dictionaries of 1600–1619.
- The spelling is colonial and the pronunciations below are **approximate reconstructions**.
- There is **no evidence of a Muisca script**. Painted cotton mantas recorded status and history, but that is "writing without words", not text ([AHA abstract](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2025/webprogram/Paper39360.html)).

### Sensitivity

- Muisca *cabildos* (Suba, Bosa, Cota, Chía, Sesquilé) are actively reviving the language. One example is [SubaCubun](https://subacubun.com/) of the Cabildo Muysca de Suba.
- **Ask them before using a word commercially.**
- **Avoid sacred terms:**
  - *chyquy*, priest;
  - *fihizca*, soul or spirit;
  - *Sua* and *Chie*, the Sun and Moon;
  - *Bochica*, *Bachué* and *Chiminigagua*.
- Other Chibchan languages: Kogi's *Aluna* stays rejected. See the Aluna subsection below.

### Aluna (asked again: free, open source, donations)

**What it is.** In Kogi thought, *aluna* is the world of thought, mind and spirit that comes before the material world and sustains it. A film press kit glosses it as "idea, consciousness, thought and essence" ([Wikipedia: Kogi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kogi_people), [*Aluna* press kit](https://www.thefilmcollaborative.org/films/img/epk/Press_Kit_Aluna_040813.pdf)). The concept is shared across the four peoples of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta: the Kogi (Kággaba), Arhuaco (Iku), Wiwa and Kankuamo. Patrícia Lora León places *Alúna* and *Sewá* at the centre of their own thought, around the principle of communication *Yuluka*, in their duty of care for all that exists ([Espaço Ameríndio, 2013](https://seer.ufrgs.br/index.php/EspacoAmerindio/article/download/38279/25963/162309)). It sits inside their *Ley de Origen* ("Law of Origin"), the highest ancestral authority that governs them ([Ministerio de Cultura](https://mng.mincultura.gov.co/prensa/noticias/Documents/Poblaciones/PUEBLO%20WIWA.pdf)).

**UNESCO.** In **2022**, UNESCO inscribed the "Ancestral system of knowledge of the four indigenous peoples, Arhuaco, Kankuamo, Kogui and Wiwa of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta" on the **Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity** ([decision 17.COM 7.b.3](https://ich.unesco.org/en/Decisions/17.COM/7.b.3)).

**Who speaks for them.**
- The **Consejo Territorial de Cabildos (CTC)** is their joint body. Colombia's Ministry of Environment recognised it in Resolution 0621 of 2002 as the main channel of dialogue between the four peoples and the state.
- It is made up of four organisations, one per people:
  - **Organización Gonawindúa Tayrona** (Kogi);
  - Confederación Indígena Tayrona (Arhuaco);
  - Organización Wiwa Yugumaiun Bunkuanarrua Tayrona (Wiwa);
  - Organización Indígena Kankuama (Kankuamo).
- Source: [Parques Nacionales management plan, 2020](https://www.parquesnacionales.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/plan-de-manejo-del-pnn-sierra-nevada-de-santa-marta-y-tayrona-1.pdf).

**The law: Andean Community Decision 486, Art. 136(g).** This is the Andean Community's industrial-property law, and Colombia's trademark office (SIC) applies it directly. It bars registering signs that:

> "consist of the name of indigenous, African American, or local communities, or of such denominations, words, letters, characters, or signs as are used to distinguish their products, services or methods of processing, or that constitute an expression of their culture or practice, **unless the application is filed by the community itself or with its express consent**"
>
> ([official English text, OAS SICE](http://www.sice.oas.org/trade/junac/decisiones/DEC486ce.asp))

What that means for us:
- The test is the sign itself, not whether we make money. A free, open-source, donation-funded app filing "Aluna" in Colombia would still fall under 136(g), and the office could refuse it or a later challenge could cancel it.
- We could use the name without registering it. But then we could not protect it, and the cultural problem remains.
- This is my reading, not legal advice.

**Existing uses.** The name is already crowded:
- Alan Ereira's 2012 documentary *Aluna*, made with the Kogi.
- [Aluna.Social](https://www.kucoin.com/price/ALN), a crypto trading platform with its ALN token.
- The singer **Aluna** (Aluna Francis, of [AlunaGeorge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AlunaGeorge)).
- aluna.app, .com and .dev are all registered.

**Recommendation: don't use it.** It is a sacred concept belonging to living peoples who guard their knowledge closely. It is UNESCO-listed. Colombian trademark law bars it without their consent. And it is already crowded.

**If the lead still wants it,** the respectful path is to ask before building anything:
1. Write to the CTC, or to the Organización Gonawindúa Tayrona for the Kogi. Explain the project, that it is free and non-commercial, and why the name matters to us.
2. Accept that the answer may be no, or may never come.
3. Only proceed with **explicit written consent**. Treat a yes as a relationship: credit them, link to them, and agree in advance how any donations are handled.

## 6. Create / creation (Tolkien, Chibcha, Inca)

### Sacred names to avoid

These are proper names of creator figures. The Andean and Muisca names are sacred to living peoples, and Tolkien's carries the most IP and religious weight of any of his words.
- **Chiminigagua**: the Muisca supreme creator, who sent birds to bring light ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiminigagua)).
- **Bachué**: the Muisca mother of humanity ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachu%C3%A9)).
- **Wiraqucha / Viracocha**: the Andean supreme creator ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viracocha)).
- **Pachakamaq**: "creator and animator of the universe"; still a living pilgrimage site and shrine name ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacha_Kamaq)).
- **Ilúvatar / Eru**: Tolkien's God.

Muisca communities and Andean ritual practice still hold these names, so using them for a product reads as appropriation. **Use the ordinary verbs, not the deity names.**

## 8. Shortlists

### Top 3 per source

| Source | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tolkien** | **Enyalië**: "memory"; nothing collides; .app and .dev free | **Sanwë**: "thought"; .app and .dev free | **Tecil**: "pen"; short |
| **Latin** | **Codicilli**: "short notes"; every domain free, .com included | **Adversaria**: Cicero's notebook of temporary notes, a scratchpad | **Scheda**: "a sheet of paper"; short; .dev free |
| **Greek** | **Hypomnemata**: Foucault's personal notebooks, the ancient knowledge base | **Mnemosynon**: "memorandum, reminder" | **Grammateion**: "writing tablet"; every domain free |
| **Chibcha** | **Ioque**: "paper"; short; .app and .dev free | **Cubun**: "word / language"; check with the cabildos first | **Pque**: "memory"; verified, but too hard to say for an app name |
| **Inca** | **Qillqa**: "writing" in both Quechua and Aymara | **Qullqa**: "storehouse", the vault | **Amuyu**: Aymara "mind, thought of the heart" |

### Overall top 5

1. **Ioque** (Muisca, "paper")
   - The literal Papirus of the Colombian highlands, and the most personal story for a Colombian team.
   - Short, easy in both languages and collision-free; ioque.app and .dev are free.
   - Costs: the pronunciation is reconstructed, and you should run it by the Muisca cabildos.
2. **Enyalië** (Quenya, "memory")
   - The best meaning on the list, and Tolkien's own word, yet obscure enough to stay clear of the Palantir crowd.
   - enyalie.app is free.
   - Costs: the dots in the spelling, and some exposure to Tolkien IP.
3. **Qillqa** (Quechua/Aymara, "writing")
   - Pan-Andean and dignified; .app and .dev are free.
   - Costs: hard to spell, and it reads "kill" in English.
4. **Codicilli** (Latin, "short notes, tablets")
   - The only candidate with **.com, .app and .dev all free**.
   - Classical, and only lawyers will think of codicils.
5. **Hypomnemata** (Greek, personal notebooks)
   - The exact concept the app implements; .app and .dev are free.
   - Costs: long and hard to say. It may suit a tagline better ("your hypomnemata").

### Use as feature names

- **Adversaria** for the scratchpad.
- **Qullqa** for the vault.
- **Khipu** for the Global View graph.
- **Tengwar** for typography or themes.
