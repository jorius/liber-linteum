# Naming brief: a new name for "Notes In My Way"

**Sources covered:** Tolkien's Elvish, Latin, Greek, Chibcha (Muisca) and Inca (Quechua and Aymara).

## How to read this

- **Research date:** 2026-10-20.
- **Meanings** come from these sources:
  - Elvish: [Eldamo](https://eldamo.org/), a lexicon that cites Tolkien's own manuscripts.
  - Latin: Lewis & Short, read on Perseus.
  - Greek: LSJ (Liddell–Scott–Jones), read on Perseus.
  - Muisca:
    - the 17th-century *Diccionario y gramática chibcha*, edited by María Stella González de Pérez (Instituto Caro y Cuervo, 1987), [as digitised in the Intercontinental Dictionary Series (IDS)](https://ids.clld.org/sources/perez1987);
    - plus a 2020 study of Muisca words for memory.
  - Aymara: IDS, contributed by Félix Layme Pairumani.
  - Quechua: the sources named in each row.
- **Domains** are written as `.com·.app·.dev`, where **T** = registered and **F** = no registration found. They come from registry lookups (RDAP) on 2026-10-20. "F" can still be a reserved or premium domain.
- **Collisions** come from quick web searches. **Nothing here is a trademark clearance.** Before you commit, search USPTO, EUIPO, SIC (Colombia) and IMPI (Mexico).
- **Pronunciation:** EN is for English speakers and ES for Spanish speakers. Stressed syllables are in CAPS.

### IP risk

**Who owns what:**
- The **Tolkien Estate** holds the literary copyright.
- **Middle-earth Enterprises** (formerly Tolkien Enterprises, under the Saul Zaentz Company) holds the trademark and merchandising rights to *The Lord of the Rings* and *The Hobbit*.
- **Embracer** bought Middle-earth Enterprises in 2022 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises)).
- In 2025 Embracer renamed the division that holds those rights **Fellowship Entertainment** ([TheOneRing.net](https://www.theonering.net/torwp/2025/06/09/120500-embracer-group-renames-itself-fellowship-entertainment/), [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/embracer-shakes-up-its-gaming-divisions-lord-of-the-rings-group-rebranded-coffee-stain-spins-off)).

**Enforcement history:**
- **2012, The Hobbit pub (Southampton):** threatened over its name, offered a licence at $100 a year, and sent another letter in 2020 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hobbit,_Southampton), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2012/03/16/hobbit_reprieved/)).
- **"Lord of the Wings":** an earlier application that Middle-earth Enterprises blocked.
- **2025, Lord of the Fries (Australia):** Middle-earth Enterprises opposed the mark and lost before IP Australia ([Variety AU](https://au.variety.com/2025/more/news/lord-of-the-fries-trademark-dispute-winner-20840)).
- **2026, a Turkish jeweller:** sued over a ring trademark ([IIPLA](https://iipla.org/news/turkish-jeweler-faces-us-lawsuit-over-lord-of-the-rings-trademark)).
- **2023, the Polychron case:** the Estate won an injunction and about $134k in fees against an unauthorised sequel. That was a copyright case, not a naming case ([report](https://westmeathexaminer.ie/?p=87933)).

**What that means for us:**
- **Ordinary Elvish nouns** like *enyalië*, *sanwë* and *tecil* carry less risk than famous proper names such as Palantír, Mithril or Rivendell.
- I found no public lawsuit against Palantir or Anduril, but that is not clearance.
- **Tengwar** and **Quenta** are the most exposed.
- Ask a lawyer before you file.

### Crowded field

Examples of Tolkien names in tech:
- **Palantir**: surveillance and data.
- **Anduril**: defense.
- **Erebor**: a bank for startups and crypto.
- **Mithril Capital** and **Lembas LLC**: Peter Thiel's funds.

## 4. Chibcha (Muisca)

### What the record is

- Muysccubun, the Muisca language, died out around the 17th–18th century.
- Everything we know comes from a few colonial dictionaries, chiefly:
  - the anonymous manuscript edited by González de Pérez (1987), digitised as [IDS](https://ids.clld.org/contributions/243);
  - and the Bogotá missionary dictionaries of 1600–1619.
- The spelling is colonial and the pronunciations below are **approximate reconstructions**.
- There is **no evidence of a Muisca script**. Painted cotton mantas recorded status and history, but that is "writing without words", not text ([AHA abstract](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2025/webprogram/Paper39360.html)).

### Sensitivity

- Muisca *cabildos* (Suba, Bosa, Cota, Chía, Sesquilé) are actively reviving the language. One example is [SubaCubun](https://subacubun.com/) of the Cabildo Muysca de Suba.
- **Ask them before using a word commercially.**
- **Avoid sacred terms:**
  - *chyquy*, priest;
  - *fihizca*, soul or spirit;
  - *Sua* and *Chie*, the Sun and Moon;
  - *Bochica*, *Bachué* and *Chiminigagua*.
- Other Chibchan languages: Kogi's *Aluna* stays rejected, because it is a living sacred concept and is tied up in the UNESCO-listed knowledge system of the Sierra Nevada.

### Overall top 5

1. **Ioque** (Muisca, "paper")
   - The literal Papirus of the Colombian highlands, and the most personal story for a Colombian team.
   - Short, easy in both languages and collision-free; ioque.app and .dev are free.
   - Costs: the pronunciation is reconstructed, and you should run it by the Muisca cabildos.
2. **Enyalië** (Quenya, "memory")
   - The best meaning on the list, and Tolkien's own word, yet obscure enough to stay clear of the Palantir crowd.
   - enyalie.app is free.
   - Costs: the dots in the spelling, and some exposure to Tolkien IP.
3. **Qillqa** (Quechua/Aymara, "writing")
   - Pan-Andean and dignified; .app and .dev are free.
   - Costs: hard to spell, and it reads "kill" in English.
4. **Codicilli** (Latin, "short notes, tablets")
   - The only candidate with **.com, .app and .dev all free**.
   - Classical, and only lawyers will think of codicils.
5. **Hypomnemata** (Greek, personal notebooks)
   - The exact concept the app implements; .app and .dev are free.
   - Costs: long and hard to say. It may suit a tagline better ("your hypomnemata").

### Use as feature names

- **Adversaria** for the scratchpad.
- **Qullqa** for the vault.
- **Khipu** for the Global View graph.
- **Tengwar** for typography or themes.
