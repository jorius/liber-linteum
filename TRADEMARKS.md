# Trademarks

The code of Liber Linteum is free software under the [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE): you
may copy it, change it, fork it and sell it, on the GPL's terms. **The name and the
icon are not part of that licence.** This page says how they may be used, so that
anyone who downloads something called Liber Linteum gets the app made here.

The GPL allows this split: section 7(e) lets a licensor decline to grant rights to
use trade names, trademarks or service marks.

## The marks

- The name **Liber Linteum**, and names confusingly similar to it.
- The **app icon** (`apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons/`, `site/img/favicon.svg`) and
  icons confusingly similar to it.

They belong to the project's authors, José Meza and Jose Ríos.

## Fine without asking

- **Unchanged copies.** Sharing, mirroring or packaging an official release (for a
  Linux distribution, Flathub, a package manager, a school's computers) under its
  own name and icon, built from an unmodified release tag. A package may patch only
  what it needs to build and run on that platform, and must say so.
- **Talking about it.** Articles, reviews, videos, tutorials, talks and comparisons
  that name Liber Linteum or show its icon to refer to the app.
- **Saying where a fork comes from.** "Based on Liber Linteum", "a fork of Liber
  Linteum" or "compatible with Liber Linteum" in a fork's description, README or
  About box, as long as its own name and icon come first and it doesn't look
  official.
- **Your own work on the project.** Development builds, pull requests and forks
  made to contribute changes back may keep the name and icon while they stay
  unpublished or clearly marked as unofficial test builds.

## Not without written permission

- **Selling or distributing a modified version under the name or the icon.** A fork
  that changes the app, or one that is sold, needs its own name and its own icon.
- **Names and icons that look like these.** Close variations ("Liber Linteum Pro",
  "LiberLinteum Plus", the same icon recoloured) count as the marks.
- **Implying it's official.** Suggesting a product, service, store listing or
  company is made, endorsed or supported by the project when it isn't.
- **Using the marks in another product's name, a domain name, an app store
  account or a company name.**

## Renaming a fork

Change the app's name in `apps/desktop/src-tauri/tauri.conf.json` (`productName`,
`identifier`), the window titles, the `site/` pages and the README, and replace
every file under `apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons/` and `site/img/`. Keep the GPL's
copyright and licence notices: those stay whatever the name.

## Asking

If you're unsure whether a use is fine, or want permission for one this page
doesn't allow, open an issue at <https://github.com/jorius/liber-linteum/issues>.
Reasonable requests, such as a community translation or a teaching build, are
welcome.

This policy may be updated; the version in this repository applies.
